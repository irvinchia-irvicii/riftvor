Your collection stays yours.

This development-stage notice explains how Soraka's Wish handles account, collection, and optional research data. It will be reviewed before public launch.

Information needed to provide the service

We store your email address, securely hashed password, saved searches, collection records, folders, purchase prices you enter, and account settings. This information provides sign-in, collection, and portfolio functions. We do not sell or disclose individual portfolios to retailers.

Optional community analytics

If you actively opt in, we record only the card identifier, finish, quantity, calendar day, and whether the activity was a search or collection addition. We do not copy search text, email, folder names, notes, prices paid, shop, location, or IP address into the analytics dataset.

Retailer reporting uses aggregate statistics only. A card signal is suppressed unless at least 20 distinct consenting users contributed during the reporting period. Searches and collection additions are labelled as interest signals, never represented as verified sales.

Your choices

Analytics contribution is optional and is not required to use an account. Change it from the Account panel at any time. Withdrawing consent immediately stops new research events and deletes your existing analytics events. It does not delete your private collection.

Retention, security, and requests

We retain account and collection information while your account is active or while reasonably needed to provide the service. Public hosting will use HTTPS, access controls, backups, a breach-response process, and a documented deletion schedule. You may request access, correction, or account deletion.
