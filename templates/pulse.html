What this page will—and won’t—measure

We can showOfficial news, upcoming events, and clearly sourced published decklists or finishes.
We won’t inferDeck play rates, win rates, matchup percentages, or a complete competitive metagame.
Every result needsAn event, date, placement, player attribution where public, and a link to its source.

This approach follows Riot’s current Riftbound developer policy. Third-party tournament sources will only be connected after their terms or permission allow it.
